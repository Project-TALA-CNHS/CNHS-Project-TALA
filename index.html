THE PROGRAM

Reading support, made intentional.

TALA brings together assessment, learner profiling, intervention, monitoring, and reading habits in one coherent school literacy framework.

01

Assess

Use reading evidence to understand learners’ speed, comprehension, decoding, and vocabulary needs.

02

Profile

Organize learners into actionable reading profiles so support can match the learner—not just the score.

03

Intervene

Provide focused reading activities, ARAL Time, monitoring, and purposeful practice through TALA SULONG.
